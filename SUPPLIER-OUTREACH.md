# Supplier outreach brief

Subject: UK plug-in solar reseller enquiry — Bright Socket

Hello,

I am preparing a UK ecommerce business focused on carefully screened plug-in solar kits for domestic customers. I would like to understand whether you support reseller or fulfilment partnerships for your plug-in range.

Please could you provide:

1. Current wholesale pricing, minimum order quantities and stock availability.
2. Complete-kit compliance evidence against the current GB plug-in solar framework, including test reports and Declaration of Conformity.
3. Confirmation of the exact AC output, current limitation, anti-islanding behaviour and manufacturer-supplied BS 1363 plug/fuse arrangement.
4. PV module, inverter, cable, connector and mounting standards/certificates.
5. Warranty length, claims process, spare parts and technical support arrangements.
6. Delivery lead times, shipping rates, returns process and damage-in-transit procedure.
7. Whether you can ship directly to customers under a reseller arrangement, and what product data/images we may use on our website.
8. Product liability insurance details and the name of the responsible manufacturer/importer for Great Britain.

We are currently building a documentation-led catalogue and will not describe products as approved until the complete-kit evidence has been reviewed.

Kind regards,

Bright Socket
