# Customer-facing claims policy

## Safe to use before verification

- “Candidate product”
- “Public specifications currently show…”
- “Supplier documentation under review”
- “Pre-launch catalogue”
- “Subject to final UK compliance checks”
- “Output limited by the stated microinverter specification”

## Use only after evidence review

- “Compliant with the GB plug-in solar Interim Product Specification”
- “Approved for sale and plug-in use under the applicable UK regulations”
- “Verified product”
- “Ready to order”
- Any statement that a specific kit is suitable for a balcony, wall, fence, roof or other mounting location

## Do not use

- “Government approved” unless a named official approval exists for that exact product/model.
- “Works in every home.”
- “No checks required.”
- “Battery compatible” on a plug-in solar product page without confirming whether the product is integrated with a battery and within the applicable scope.
- Savings or payback promises without a stated methodology, assumptions and disclaimer.

## Product-page release gate

A product can move from “candidate” to “verified” only when the tracker contains the exact model/revision, complete-kit test evidence, declaration of conformity, plug/fuse evidence, inverter/export evidence, mounting evidence, ENA Type Test Register record, warranty route, responsible economic operator and fulfilment terms.
