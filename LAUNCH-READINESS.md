# Bright Socket launch-readiness checklist

## Research and regulatory

- [x] Identify the GB plug-in solar legal route and commencement milestone.
- [x] Record the Interim Product Specification limits and exclusions.
- [x] Update the project to the final Interim Product Specification.
- [x] Separate public supplier marketing claims from complete-kit evidence.
- [ ] Confirm the final applicable guidance and any DNO/customer notification process immediately before launch.

## Product and supplier

- [x] Create a four-SKU candidate catalogue.
- [x] Record public specifications and observed retail prices.
- [x] Prepare model-specific supplier questions.
- [ ] Receive complete-kit test evidence for each SKU.
- [ ] Confirm the exact model is registered on the ENA Type Test Register before placing it on the market.
- [ ] Receive plug, fuse and contact-protection evidence.
- [ ] Receive mounting/stability evidence for each declared use.
- [ ] Confirm responsible economic operator and Declaration of Conformity.
- [ ] Agree wholesale, fulfilment, returns, warranty and damage-in-transit terms.
- [ ] Confirm product-liability insurance arrangements.

## Website and customer experience

- [x] Build responsive catalogue homepage.
- [x] Add candidate product cards and one evidence-trail page.
- [x] Add suitability checker with GB/NI and battery-scope warnings.
- [x] Add safety FAQ and claims policy.
- [x] Add privacy-notice draft and consent checkbox.
- [x] Keep payments disabled while products are unverified.
- [ ] Replace draft privacy notice with final business/provider details.
- [ ] Add final brand identity, legal entity, contact details and terms.
- [ ] Add verified product pages and payment/fulfilment flow.
- [ ] Publish only after a final content and compliance review.

## Recommended order of work

1. Send the supplier outreach brief to UKSOL first, then iHelios and UK Solar Kits.
2. Choose one initial SKU and fulfilment method; avoid launching four products at once.
3. Move that SKU through the evidence tracker and claims release gate.
4. Replace the candidate product page with a verified product page only after the evidence is complete.
5. Add payment and fulfilment only after the supplier agreement and legal business details are settled.
