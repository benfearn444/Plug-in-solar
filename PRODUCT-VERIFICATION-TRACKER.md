## ENA public-register check

An open-web search on 15 August 2026 found no indexed ENA Type Test Register result for the model references currently recorded above, including `UKS-PRO-D-2-890-MZ1`. This is not proof that a model is absent from the register; the supplier must provide the exact ENA record or searchable model reference before the product can pass the release gate.

## Evidence required for each row

- Exact model number and revision supplied to UK customers.
- Test report mapping the product to the current GB plug-in solar specification.
- Declaration of Conformity and responsible manufacturer/importer details.
- Confirmation of maximum apparent power and maximum mains current.
- Confirmation of the manufacturer-supplied BS 1363-1 moulded plug, BS 1362 fuse and contact protection.
- Inverter anti-islanding and G98 evidence.
- PV module, DC cable and connector conformity evidence.
- Mounting stability calculations and declared mounting locations.
- Warranty, product liability insurance, returns process and customer support contact.
- Confirmation of whether the kit contains or is designed to connect to a battery; battery-integrated products are outside the IPS scope.
- For kits above 960W total PV input, confirmation that the manufacturer gives the required professional-assessment advice.
- Confirmation that any declared outdoor socket/plug arrangement reaches IP55 and that the instructions prohibit multiway adaptors and RCD adaptors.
- ENA Type Test Register record for the exact device/model before it is placed on the market.

No candidate should be shown as “approved” or accepted for payment until every applicable item has been reviewed and recorded.

## Public evidence observed

The UKSOL 890W and 1260W public datasheets state a 799 VA maximum continuous output and 3.5 A maximum output current, which align with the IPS limits. They also list inverter and PV standards such as BS EN 50549-1 and BS EN 62109-1/-2. This is useful evidence, but it is not the same as a complete-kit IPS conformity assessment. The public datasheets do not state the required BS 1363-1 moulded plug and BS 1362 fuse details, so those remain supplier questions.

The UK Solar Kits ESK400 page states that the kit includes a UK plug cable, but it does not publicly provide enough detail to verify the IPS plug, fuse, contact protection, test reports or mounting calculations.
