Before you plug in

Good questions deserve straight answers.

Can these kits be used on a balcony?

No. The current UKSOL product data says this range is not suitable for balcony installation. We are concentrating on suitable ground-level garden and patio positions.

Is ballast included?

No. UKSOL’s supplied price list says ballast is not included. The product page will make all required extras clear before orders open.

Can I add a plug-in battery?

Not to this launch range. We will only introduce battery products once their compatibility, product route and warranty position are confirmed.

Will every home be suitable?

No. A qualified electrician must install and certify a suitable outdoor socket. Customers must also follow the supplied instructions and check their available space.
