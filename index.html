Before you plug in

Good questions deserve straight answers.

Are the products approved yet?

Not on this pre-launch catalogue. We will only use an approved/compliant sales label after the complete kit and supplier evidence have been checked against the applicable UK requirements.

Can I add a plug-in battery?

The current plug-in solar specification is for solar products and excludes plug-in batteries and solar devices integrated with batteries. We will not include those products in this range.

Is the 800W figure the panel output?

No. The important connection limit is the inverter’s apparent power delivered to the home. A kit can have more panel input for better low-light performance while still limiting AC export, but the exact product evidence must be checked.

Will every home be suitable?

No. Customers need a suitable, stable position, a suitable socket and a home electrical installation that can safely accept the product. We will publish suitability guidance and the manufacturer’s instructions with every verified listing.
